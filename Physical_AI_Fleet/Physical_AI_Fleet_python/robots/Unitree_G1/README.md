# Multi_Agent_Industrial_Twin

Physical AI project for the NVIDIA x Nebius hackathon: a Go2 inspects a faulty pump and raises a work order, an AMR (digital twin) brings the tools to the fault location, and a Unitree G1 humanoid goes with the worker, guides the repair by voice from SOPs, identifies objects with its camera, and follows the worker.

## Parts
- **G1 voice guidance agent:** [g1/voice_guidance_agent](g1/voice_guidance_agent/README.md) (Parakeet, Nemotron, Cosmos Reason on a real G1)
- Go2 inspection, work order and AMR digital twin: add links to your teammates' folders here

## NVIDIA open models used
Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2, Nemotron Nano 9B v2, Cosmos Reason 1 7B
