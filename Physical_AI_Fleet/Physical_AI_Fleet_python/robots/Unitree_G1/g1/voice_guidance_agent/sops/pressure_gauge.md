# Pressure Gauge Fault
Tools: calibration kit, adjustable wrench, spare gauge
Safety: isolate and depressurize the line first.
1. Close the isolation valve to the gauge.
2. Open the bleed valve and confirm the line is at zero.
3. Compare the faulty gauge with the calibration kit reference.
4. If the error is above 2 percent, unscrew the gauge with the adjustable wrench.
5. Fit the spare gauge and open the isolation valve slowly.
