# Valve Leak Repair
Tools: adjustable wrench, PTFE tape, torque wrench, gas detector
Safety: isolate line, depressurize, check gas detector reads zero, wear gloves and goggles.
1. Close upstream and downstream isolation valves.
2. Open bleed valve and confirm pressure gauge reads zero.
3. Loosen gland nut with the adjustable wrench.
4. Replace the packing and wrap threads with PTFE tape.
5. Tighten gland nut to 25 Nm with the torque wrench.
6. Slowly reopen the valves and check for leaks.
